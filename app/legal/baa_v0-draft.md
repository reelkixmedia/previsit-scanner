BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT — DRAFT v0 (NOT FOR USE WITH REAL PATIENT DATA UNTIL REVIEWED BY A LAWYER)

Between the practice signing below ("Covered Entity") and Previsit ("Business Associate").
Modeled on the provisions listed in 45 CFR 164.504(e) and the HHS sample business associate agreement provisions.

1. Purpose. Business Associate provides software that checks upcoming appointments for expired prescriptions and
   overdue lab work and helps the practice contact patients. It may use and disclose protected health information
   (PHI) only to provide these services, as this agreement allows, or as required by law.

2. Safeguards. Business Associate will use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, and comply
   with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 for electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure not allowed by this agreement.

3. Reporting. Business Associate will report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not allowed by this agreement,
   any security incident, and any breach of unsecured PHI as required by 45 CFR 164.410, without unreasonable delay
   and no later than [__] days after discovery.

4. Subcontractors. Business Associate will make sure any subcontractor that creates, receives, keeps or sends PHI
   for it agrees in writing to the same restrictions and conditions.

5. Individual rights. Business Associate will make PHI available for access (164.524) and amendment (164.526), and
   provide the information needed for an accounting of disclosures (164.528), within [__] days of a request.

6. Covered Entity's obligations. Where Business Associate carries out an obligation of Covered Entity under
   Subpart E, it will comply with the requirements that apply to Covered Entity for that obligation.

7. Government access. Business Associate will make its internal practices, books and records available to the
   Secretary of HHS to determine compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

8. Minimum necessary. Business Associate will request, use and disclose only the minimum PHI necessary.

9. Term and termination. This agreement lasts while Business Associate provides services. Covered Entity may end it
   if Business Associate breaks a material term and does not fix it within [__] days.

10. Return or destruction. When this agreement ends, Business Associate will return or destroy all PHI it holds and
    keep no copies, or, if that is not feasible, extend these protections to the PHI and limit further use.

11. Interpretation. Any unclear wording will be read to allow compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

Signed electronically. Typing your name and selecting "Sign" has the same effect as a handwritten signature.
